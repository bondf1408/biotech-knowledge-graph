Added GEN-hp4d-YL-001 (inducible hp4d promoter) reference to the "зависит от" section and related_cards array of all six enzyme kinetics cards (ENZ-OLS, ENZ-OAC, ENZ-CsPT4, ENZ-THCS, ENZ-ACC1, ENZ-ERG20) as part of Block 9 expression regulation work.

**Why:** To maintain comprehensive cross-referencing between genetic modification cards and enzyme kinetics cards, documenting available promoter systems for Y. lipolytica expression systems.

**How to apply:** When designing expression constructs for cannabinoid pathway enzymes in Y. lipolytica, both TEF1 (constitutive strong) and hp4d (inducible) promoters are now documented options in the knowledge base, enabling researchers to choose appropriate expression strategies based on their production timeline and metabolic burden considerations.
