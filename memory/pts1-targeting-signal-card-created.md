Created GEN-PTS1-YL-001 card documenting peroxisomal targeting signal (PTS1) approach for compartmentalizing cannabinoid pathway enzymes in peroxisomes of Yarrowia lipolytica to access high acetyl-CoA pools. Includes consensus sequence details, mechanism, benefits, limitations, and application strategies for OLS, OAC, ACC1, and THCAS enzymes.

**Why:** Peroxisomal compartmentalization offers access to elevated acetyl-CoA pools from β-oxidation, potential reduction of product inhibition, and co-localization of pathway enzymes to improve metabolic flux toward cannabinoid production.

**How to apply:** Add canonical PTS1 signal (SKL or variants like AKL) to C-terminus of target enzymes (OLS, OAC, ACC1, THCAS) to direct their expression to peroxisomes. Consider dual-targeting signals for simultaneous localization to multiple compartments. Optimize expression conditions to induce peroxisome proliferation via oleic acid feeding and monitor peroxisomal markers (catalase, ACOX) to assess induction levels.
