Created GEN-POX2-YL-001 card documenting the methanol-inducible POX2 promoter from Yarrowia lipolytica for heterologous expression, particularly useful for peroxisomally-localized enzymes in the cannabinoid pathway. Includes promoter strength, induction characteristics, synergy with PTS1 targeting signals, and application strategies for cannabinoid pathway enzymes.

**Why:** The POX2 promoter provides a strong methanol-inducible expression system (50-100× induction) that synergizes with peroxisomal targeting signals (PTS1) to drive expression of cannabinoid pathway enzymes in peroxisomes, providing access to high acetyl-CoA pools from β-oxidation and enabling two-stage cultivation (growth on glucose → production on methanol).

**How to apply:** Use the POX2 promoter for inducible expression of peroxisomally-localized cannabinoid pathway enzymes (OLS, OAC, ACC1, THCAS with PTS1 signals) in Y. lipolytica. Combine with PTS1 targeting signals for peroxisomal localization, codon optimization (GEN-CODON-OPT-YL-001) for enhanced expression, and fed-batch methanol feeding strategies for controlled induction. Monitor peroxisomal markers (catalase, ACOX) to assess induction levels and mitigate oxidative stress.
