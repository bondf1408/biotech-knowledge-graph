# THCVA and DVA production titers in Yarrowia lipolytica

## Direct cannabinoid titers (limited reports)
- No direct THCVA or DVA titer data found in the current literature search (as of 2026-06-03). Most studies report olivetolic acid (OLA) and cannabigerolic acid (CBGA) as precursors.

## Precursor titers (indicative of pathway flux)
- **Olivetolic acid (OLA)**:
  - Peak titer: 9.84 mg/L (YX112 strain + 1 mM hexanoic acid addition day 1) [[MED-007:L71]]
  - Stabilized titer: 7.64 mg/L (by day 6) [[MED-007:L72]]
- **Cannabigerolic acid (CBGA)**:
  - Titer: 3.537 mg/L (YX112 + hexanoic acid, day 6) [[MED-007:L73]]
- **Olivetolic acid (OSA)** (glucose-only carbon source, YX206 strain):
  - Titer: 18.87 mg/L (final titer day 5-6) [[MED-007:L74]]
- **Cannabigerolic acid (CBGOA)** (YX206, glucose-only):
  - Titer: 541 µg/L (day 5) [[MED-007:L75]]

## Fermentation conditions for precursor production
- **Carbon source**: Glucose ≥30 g/L maintained throughout fermentation (YX206) [[MED-007:L76]]
- **Hexanoic acid addition**: 1 mM on day 1 (for YX112) [[MED-007:L77]]
- **Lipid content**: 35.8% (glucose-fed batch) [[MED-007:L78]]
- **β-carotene production** (as pathway indicator):
  - 63.3 mg/g dry weight (glucose-fed batch) [[MED-007:L81]]
  - 202 mg/L (glycerol-fed batch) [[MED-007:L82]]
- **Lycopene production**:
  - 85.6 mg/L (glycerol-fed batch + CrtW/CrtZ expression) [[MED-007:L83]]
- **Astaxanthin production**:
  - 120.4 mg/L (glycerol-fed batch + beta-carotene hydroxylase/ketolase) [[MED-007:L84]]

## Expected THCVA/DVA yields (inferred from precursor data)
- Based on OLA to THCVA conversion via OAC and THCAS, theoretical maximum THCVA yield from OLA is ~1:1 molar ratio (OLA 224 g/mol → THCVA 286 g/mol).
- Reported OLA titers suggest potential THCVA titers in the range of 5-15 mg/L if pathway is fully engineered and optimized.
- Multiplicative improvements reported: DVA production in fed-batch vs batch shows 3-5× increase [[MED-007:L66]].

**Why**: Direct THCVA/DVA titer data is scarce; precursor titers (OLA, CBGA) and pathway indicator carotenoids provide a proxy for engineering progress. Improvements in these metrics correlate with enhanced cannabinoid potential.

**How to apply**: When evaluating Y. lipolytica strains for THCVA/DVA production, monitor OLA and CBGA titers as early indicators. Carotenoid titers (β-carotene, lycopene, astaxanthin) can serve as secondary screens for terpenoid pathway and NADPH availability.
