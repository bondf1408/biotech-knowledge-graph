# Carotenoid production titers in Yarrowia lipolytica

## Beta-carotene
- **Titer**: 202 mg/L (fed-batch with glycerol) [[MED-007:L82]]
- **Titer**: 63.3 mg/g dry weight (fed-batch with glucose) [[MED-007:L81]]

## Lycopene
- **Titer**: 85.6 mg/L (fed-batch with glycerol, expressing CrtW and CrtZ) [[MED-007:L83]]

## Astaxanthin
- **Titer**: 120.4 mg/L (fed-batch with glycerol, expressing beta-carotene hydroxylase and ketolase) [[MED-007:L84]]

**Why**: These carotenoid titers serve as measurable indicators of flux through the terpenoid pathway (MEP/MVA) and redox balance (NADPH availability), which are critical for cannabinoid precursor supply in Y. lipolytica.

**How to apply**: Use carotenoid production as a screening metric when engineering Y. lipolytica for cannabinoid biosynthesis; improvements in beta-carotene, lycopene, or astaxanthin titers suggest enhanced isoprenoid precursor and NADPH supply beneficial for THCVA/DVA production.
