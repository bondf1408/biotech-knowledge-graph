Created GEN-CODON-OPT-YL-001 card documenting codon optimization approaches for improving heterologous gene expression in Yarrowia lipolytica for cannabinoid biosynthesis. Includes details on Y. lipolytica codon usage preferences, optimization process, expected benefits, and integration with other expression regulation strategies.

**Why:** Codon optimization is a key strategy for improving expression levels of heterologous enzymes in the cannabinoid pathway, directly impacting metabolic flux and final product titers.

**How to apply:** When designing expression constructs for cannabinoid pathway enzymes (OLS, OAC, CsPT4, THCS, ACC1, ERG20) in Y. lipolytica, apply codon optimization using Y. lipolytica-preferred codons, particularly favoring G/C-ending codons and avoiding rare tRNA combinations. Combine with promoter selection (TEF1/hp4d) and targeting signals (PTS1) for maximal expression improvement.
