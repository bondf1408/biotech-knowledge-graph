## Detailed Interactions

### ACC1 Overexpression (ACC1↑)
- ↑ Malonyl-CoA production (direct product of ACC1)
- ↑ OLS/OAC activity (more malonyl-CoA substrate)
- ↑ THCAS activity (more CBGVA precursor via OLS/OAC)
- ↑ OLA, DVA, CBGVA, THCVA titers (increased precursor flux)
- ↔ Propionyl-CoA levels (different pathway)
- Potential ↓ due to acetyl-CoA drain (competing with fatty acid synthesis)

### Malonyl-CoA Increase
- ↓ ACC1 activity (feedback inhibition)
- ↑↑ OLS/OAC activity (substrate availability)
- ↑ Polyketide titers (OLA, DVA, CBGVA)
- ↑ THCVA via increased CBGVA
- ↔ Propionyl-CoA levels

### Propionyl-CoA Increase
- ↑ DVA production (starter unit for OLS/OAC with C3)
- ↑ CBGVA production (via OLS/OAC with C3 starter)
- ↑ THCVA (via THCAS on CBGVA)
- ↔ Malonyl-CoA levels
- ↔ ACC1 activity

### OLS/OAC Overexpression
- ↑ Malonyl-CoA consumption (increased flux)
- ↑ Acetyl-CoA consumption (via ACC1 to make malonyl-CoA)
- ↑ OLA production (C6 starter)
- ↑ DVA production (C3 starter if propionyl-CoA available)
- ↑ CBGVA production (from DVA prenylation)
- ↑ THCVA production (from CBGVA)
- ↑ ACC1 activity (to replenish malonyl-CoA pool)
- ↑ Propionyl-CoA utilization (if available for DVA)

### THCAS Overexpression
- ↑ THCVA production (from CBGVA)
- ↑ CBGVA consumption
- ↑ OLA/DVA indirectly (via precursor pull-through)
- ↑ Malonyl-CoA consumption (to make more CBGVA)
- ↑ Propionyl-CoA consumption (for CBGVA synthesis)
- ↑ ACC1 activity (to replenish malonyl-CoA)
- ↑ OLS/OAC activity (to replenish CBGVA)

### Knockdown/Depletion Effects
- ACC1 knockdown → ↓ malonyl-CoA → ↓ all polyketides
- Malonyl-CoA depletion → ↓ OLS/OAC activity → ↓ OLA/DVA/CBGVA/THCVA
- Propionyl-CoA depletion → ↓ DVA/CBGVA → ↓ THCVA (but OLA unaffected)

### MVA Pathway Enhancement (MVA↑)
- ↑ IPP/DMAPP pools (direct products of MVA pathway)
- ↑ CBGVA production (via increased prenylation of DVA)
- ↑ THCVA production (via increased CBGVA substrate for THCAS)
- ↔ OLA Production (different pathway - polyketide vs terpenoid)
- ↔ DVA Production (different pathway - polyketide vs terpenoid)
- Potential ↓ due to acetyl-CoA drain (competing with polyketide pathway for acetyl-CoA)
- Potential ↓ due to ATP drain (kinases steps require ATP)

### Ketoyl-CoA Thiolase Upregulation (Ketoyl-CoA Thiolas↑)
- ↓ OLA Production (via increased cleavage of C6-acetoacetyl-CoA to 2x acetyl-CoA)
- ↔ DVA Production (propionyl-CoA starter less affected by C6 thiolase activity)
- ↔ CBGVA Production (depends on balance of precursors)
- ↔ THCVA Production (depends on balance of precursors)
- Potential ↑ shorter chain polyketides (C4 products)
- Potential ↓ longer chain polyketides (if thiolase prefers longer chains)

### Knockdown/Depletion Effects
- ACC1 knockdown → ↓ malonyl-CoA → ↓ all polyketides
- Malonyl-CoA depletion → ↓ OLS/OAC activity → ↓ OLA/DVA/CBGVA/THCVA
- Propionyl-CoA depletion → ↓ DVA/CBGVA → ↓ THCVA (but OLA unaffected)
- MVA pathway knockdown → ↓ IPP/DMAPP → ↓ CBGVA → ↓ THCVA (but OLA/DVA unaffected)
- Ketoyl-CoA thiolase knockdown → ↑ OLA (reduced cleavage of C6-intermediates) → potential ↓ shorter chain products

## Sources
- Based on pathway biochemistry: acetyl-CoA → (ACC1) → malonyl-CoA → (OLS) → ketide → (OAC) → olivetolic acid (OLA) or divarinic acid (DVA) with propionyl-CoA starter
- OLA/DVA → (GAT) → CBGVA/CBGA → (THCAS) → THCVA/THCA
- Acetyl-CoA → (AACS) → acetoacetyl-CoA → (HMGS) → HMG-CoA → (HMGR) → mevalonate → ... → IPP/DMAPP (MVA pathway)
- Ketoyl-CoA + CoA ←(thiolase)→ acetyl-CoA + acyl-CoA (reverse Claisen condensation)
- Feedback inhibition of ACC1 by malonyl-CoA documented in many organisms
- Feedback inhibition of HMGR by mevalonate/documents in many organisms
- Substrate specificity of OLS/OAC for acetyl-CoA vs propionyl-CoA starter units
- Thiolase specificity for different acyl-CoA chain lengths

## Notes
- Interactions assume sufficient cofactor availability (Mg²⁺ for ACC1/OAC, FAD/NADPH for THCAS, NADPH for HMGR)
- Actual magnitudes depend on expression levels, promoter strength, and cultivation conditions
- Competition for acetyl-CoA with fatty acid biosynthesis may modulate ACC1 overexpression benefits
- Competition for acetyl-CoA between MVA pathway and polyketide/fatty acid synthesis may modulate benefits
- Thiolase effects depend on isoform specificity and subcellular localization
